Hybrid: a string whose bridge is a metal bar

Experimental. Everything you hear is the physical model's sound pressure in front of the soundboard; nothing is sampled, filtered, compressed or faded. Nobody has listened to this instrument yet: the judgement is yours.

The dB beside each strength is the model's own loudness for that pluck or stroke (A-weighted, 1 m in front of the board), so the two can be set against each other. Plucks run from about 55 to 79 dB and strokes from about 69 to 89 dB: they overlap between about 69 and 79 dB (a pluck of 2.5 N or more against a stroke of 0.7 m/s or less). The page starts there, at 4 N and 0.5 m/s. Nothing is done to the sound to bring them together.

Keys

A pluck · S strike at the slider's strength · 1 2 3 gentle, medium, strong strike · hold M to mute the string · hold D to damp the bar. The two fingers are independent: either can be held, lifted or moved while the other is. The Mute and Damp buttons stay down until pressed again; the keys act while held; a finger is down while either says so. After choosing from a list the keys go back to the instrument.

How to listen: a short procedure
  1. Start audio. Set the volume with a medium strike (2) so that it is comfortable, and leave it there for steps 2 to 8. The page starts with a 4 N pluck and a 0.5 m/s stroke, which the model puts within 3 dB of each other; harder strokes are louder than any pluck.
  2. Approved string instrument. Choose "Approved string on its body". Pluck (A) a few times, position 0.8.
  3. Bar-as-bridge reference. Choose "Hybrid: string tied at 0.20". The same plucks. Then try pluck positions 0.5, 0.75 and 0.9.
  4. Attachment 0.20 against 0.18. Pluck, then strike (2) and let it ring; change to "tied at 0.18" and do the same.
  5. Bar without damping. Tied at 0.20: strike (2) and let it die away.
  6. Bar with damping. Strike and, a moment later, hold D. Then hold D first and strike. Try the three damping positions and strengths.
  7. Gentle and medium strikes. 1, then 2; then 3.
  8. Pluck and strike together. Press A and 2 at once. Then again and hold D (the bar stops, the string goes on), or hold M (the string stops, the bar goes on), or both.
  9. If you want to compare plucks closely, raise the volume for them and remember to lower it before striking.

What to listen for (and note down; nobody has judged any of this yet):

  • String tone. Is the hybrid's plucked string still a string you would want? How does it differ from the approved one, at each pluck position?
  • Metallic resonance. Is the bar heard in a pluck? In a stroke, is its note clear, and does it sit well with the string's pitch?
  • Pluck and strike balance. At 4 N and 0.5 m/s, and across each slider: over what range can the two be played together?
  • Damping response. Does D stop the bar as a hand would? Which position and strength? Does M feel different from D?
  • Attachment. Tied at 0.20 against 0.18: is there a difference worth having, in the pluck and in the stroke's tail?
  • Latency. Does the sound follow the key or the tap closely enough to play in time? (The diagnostics give the browser's estimate.)
  • Clicks or distortion. Any click when a finger lands or lifts, when notes repeat fast, or when switching instruments? Any harshness that the "clipped" warning in the diagnostics explains, or does not?

This page does not say which is better. The model's sound pressure is not calibrated; your loudspeakers or headphones are part of what you hear.
