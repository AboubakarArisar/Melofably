Hybrid lab: a string whose bridge is a metal bar

Experimental (Phase 3D.12). Separate from the four approved instruments and from the builder, which it does not touch. Keys

A pluck · S strike at the slider's strength · 1 2 3 gentle, medium, strong strike · hold M to mute the string · hold D to damp the bar. The two fingers are independent: either can be held, lifted or moved while the other is. The Mute and Damp buttons stay down until pressed again; the keys act while held.

How to listen: a short procedure
  1. Start audio. Set the volume with a medium strike (2) so that it is comfortable, and leave it there for steps 2 to 8. Plucks will then be quiet: that is how much quieter a pluck is than a stroke on this instrument.
  2. Approved string instrument. Choose "Approved string on its body". Pluck (A) a few times at strength 2 N, position 0.8.
  3. Bar-as-bridge reference. Choose "Hybrid: string tied at 0.20". The same plucks. Then try pluck positions 0.5, 0.75 and 0.9.
  4. Attachment 0.20 against 0.18. Pluck, then strike (2) and let it ring; change to "tied at 0.18" and do the same.
  5. Bar without damping. Tied at 0.20: strike (2) and let it die away.
  6. Bar with damping. Strike and, a moment later, hold D. Then hold D first and strike. Try the three damping positions and strengths.
  7. Gentle and medium strikes. 1, then 2; then 3.
  8. Pluck and strike together. Press A and 2 at once. Then again and hold D (the bar stops, the string goes on), or hold M (the string stops, the bar goes on), or both.
  9. If you want to compare plucks closely, raise the volume for them and remember to lower it before striking.

This page does not say which is better. The model's sound pressure is not calibrated; your loudspeakers or headphones are part of what you hear.
